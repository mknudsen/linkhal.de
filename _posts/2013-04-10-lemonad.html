Lemonad is a JavaScript library supporting functional programming inspired by Clojure, ClojureScript, Functional JavaScript, ML, Haskell, Forth and Underscore. It requires the presence of either Underscore or Lo-Dash.

https://fogus.github.io/lemonad/
